# 🌾 Farmer–Customer Agricultural Marketplace

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Farmer–Customer Agricultural Marketplace** is a web-based platform designed to connect farmers directly with customers and traders. It provides a digital marketplace where farmers can list their agricultural products, while customers can browse available crops, compare prices, and send offers.

The main goal of this project is to simplify agricultural trading, improve communication between farmers and buyers, and make crop purchasing more transparent and convenient.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Connect farmers and customers through a single platform.
- Allow farmers to list and manage their agricultural products.
- Enable customers and traders to search and explore available crops.
- Provide crop details such as quality, quantity, price, and location.
- Allow buyers to submit offers to farmers.
- Help farmers review offers and accept the most suitable one.
- Improve transparency and efficiency in agricultural trading.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Module
- Register and manage an account.
- Add and manage crop listings.
- Provide crop details, including category, quality, quantity, price, location, expected availability date, description, and images.
- View offers submitted by customers and traders.
- Accept a suitable offer.
- Update crop availability and mark products as sold.

### 🛒 Customer/Trader Module
- Register and manage an account.
- Browse available agricultural products.
- Search and filter crops according to relevant criteria.
- View crop details and compare available options.
- Submit offers to farmers.
- Track the status of submitted offers.

### 🛡️ Admin Module
- Manage platform users.
- Monitor crop listings and marketplace activities.
- Help maintain the reliability and proper functioning of the platform.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Farmer lists crops:** The farmer adds crop information and sets the asking price.
2. **Buyer explores products:** Customers or traders browse available crops and review their details.
3. **Buyer submits an offer:** The buyer sends a price offer to the farmer.
4. **Farmer reviews offers:** The farmer evaluates the received offers and chooses a suitable one.
5. **Crop status is updated:** The listing is updated according to the crop's availability or sale status.
